01 — Presence

Code, curiosity,
and the moment it clicks.

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Temasek Polytechnic (Class of 2026), and I'm genuinely excited by anything to do with Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Computer Vision. There's something special about teaching a machine to see and understand the world, and that's the space I hope to specialize in.

I love coding. Whether it's building predictive models, wrangling data into clear visualizations, or just tinkering with a new idea in Python, I enjoy the process of figuring things out and making something work. I'm also a big believer in using AI as an everyday tool, and I happily use it to learn faster, work smarter, and solve problems throughout life, not just in code.

Something that matters a lot to me is teaching. I love helping others understand tricky concepts, and nothing beats the moment when something finally "clicks" for someone. Sharing what I know is one of my favourite ways to learn, too.
